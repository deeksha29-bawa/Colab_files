## 🎯 Key Topics Covered

- **Machine Learning for Finance** — Classification, anomaly detection, and risk modeling
- **Fairness & Bias Auditing** — Detecting demographic bias in credit and lending models
- **Explainability (XAI)** — SHAP values, LIME explanations, and feature importance
- **Model Transparency** — Auditing black-box models for regulatory compliance
- **Real-World Use Cases** — Fraud detection, market risk, and loan approval systems
